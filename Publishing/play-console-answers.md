# Console form answers

State the basis for every answer first, then answer. If the basis stops
being true — an ad network appears, analytics are added, a leaderboard
goes in — these answers change and data safety has to be resubmitted.

## Basis

What the game actually does, verified against the build rather than
remembered: networking, engine services and whether they are disabled,
advertising code, storage and exactly which keys, and the permission
list in the built artifact.

## Target audience and content

Age groups, and whether the app appeals to children by design. Selecting
groups under 13 brings a separate set of family-policy requirements.

## Declarations

Ads, restricted permissions, background location, health, finance,
government, news, and anything else the console asks. For restricted
permissions, point at the verified permission list rather than asserting
none are used.

## Privacy policy

URL, and which console field it goes in. The page must stay reachable
after publication — a 404 is grounds for removal.

## Developer contacts

Support email is required and publicly visible; it should match the
contact in the privacy policy. Keep it distinct from the console account
address.
